## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an exquisite, serene digital atelier for high-end modest fashion, drawing directly from the architectural precision and poised restraint of the brand mark. The personality embodies understated refinement, organic warmth, deliberate silence, and artisanal prestige. Designed for an international, discerning clientele seeking bespoke abayas, timeless tailoring, and artisanal draping, the interface evokes the hushed tranquility of a private salon in Muscat or Paris.

The visual direction centers on **Warm Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Natural light, organic warmth, and tactile breathing room. Rather than cold stark starkness or dark heavy luxury, the interface breathes through sunlit ecru, warm alabaster linen, and grounded botanical olive tones.
- **Poise & Architectural Symmetry:** Framed compositions, nested hairpins, and measured proportion drawn from the geometry of the central monogram.
- **Quiet Luxury:** Complete avoidance of aggressive marketing widgets, loud badges, or synthetic saturation. Metallic accents are subtle, restrained to brushed antique champagne and muted pale bronze.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from natural botanical dyes, woven raw linens, and patinated precious metals, creating a tranquil, high-end sensory palette.

- **Primary (`#283626` - Deep Cypress Olive):** The anchor of the brand identity and primary typographic tone. It provides an authoritative, grounding contrast against warm grounds while remaining softer and more organic than pure black.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Brushed Champagne Gold):** A warm, restrained metallic hue used for delicate accentuation, curated badges, refined dividers, and subtle active focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#6C7A67` - Muted Laurel Sage):** A mid-tone botanical green used for secondary metadata, subtle hover states, tertiary indicators, and soft graphic infills.
- **Neutral Ground (`#F5F2EB` - Warm Ecru Canvas):** The foundational backdrop of the entire experience. It radiates the tactile softness of raw silk, warm stone, and unbleached handmade paper.
- **Supporting Tonal Neutrals:** `#FAF8F5` provides pristine elevated card surfaces, while `#EDE8DF` offers grounded structural contrast for subtle backdrops, drawers, and nested containers. Hairline structural rules utilize `#D8D3C5` to define architecture without disrupting visual calm.

## Typography

The typographic treatment establishes a quiet conversation between traditional couture bookmaking and contemporary geometric clarity:

- **Editorial Expression (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for display titles, collection chapters, and poetic brand narratives. The letterforms exude delicacy and high-contrast grace, honoring classical luxury fashion publications. It should frequently be typeset with generous leading and natural tracking.
- **Architectural Clarity (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Anchors product metadata, navigational headers, bespoke tailoring specifications, and extended narrative copy. Its clean, warm geometric structure guarantees optimal readability without competing with the headline serif.
- **Couture Spatial Cadence:** Labels, sub-headers, and navigation elements (`label-md`, `label-sm`) must always be formatted in uppercase with dramatic letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.22em`), evoking the engraved lettering on luxury fashion houses' stationery and garment tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the curated pacing of an architectural gallery and physical fashion folio:

- **Grid Architecture:** A responsive 12-column layout on desktop (`1280px+`) transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a structured 4-column framework on mobile devices (`< 768px`).
- **Pacing & Generous Negative Space:** White space is treated as an active luxury material. Rather than compressing items to fill viewports, large gutters and generous section margins (`space-xl` and combined padding tiers up to 6rem–8rem) give silhouettes and bespoke fabrics ample breathing space.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Editorial collections employ intentional rhythmic variations—alternating between full-bleed hero visuals, centered single-column contemplative essays, and staggered dual-card pairings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through tonal layering, structural hairpins, and delicate atmospheric luminescence, completely rejecting artificial drop shadows.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces layer quietly above the base `#F5F2EB` canvas using `#FAF8F5` for focal cards, product showcases, and modular inspection blocks, with `#EDE8DF` providing gentle inset depth for footers, filter sidebars, and contextual drawers.
- **Architectural Framing (The Monogram Motif):** Directly referencing the nested box structure of the brand mark, components and featured visuals use 1px nested hairpins (`#D8D3C5` and `#C5A880` at 30% opacity). This creates deliberate physical framing reminiscent of fine museum vitrines and archival boxes.
- **Subtle Ambient Diffusion:** For elevated flyouts, bag sheets, and mobile menus, use an imperceptible warm amber-tinted shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(40, 54, 38, 0.06)`. All depth remains organic, sunlit, and softly grounded.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language across every component.

- **Tailored Precision:** All buttons, cards, visual frames, popovers, form controls, and dialogue sheets have strict `0px` border-radii. This echoes the crisp lines of tailored lapels, crisp textile edges, pattern paper, and the geometric double-square of the brand mark.
- **Exceptions:** Pure circles are reserved exclusively for fabric color swatches and minimal pagination dots (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button:** Deep olive green fill (`#283626`) with warm ecru typography (`#F5F2EB`), 0px border-radius, `label-md` tracking (`0.18em`), and generous padding (`1rem 2.25rem`). On hover, transitions effortlessly to `#1E281C` with subtle scale stability.
- **Secondary / Framing CTA:** Transparent surface framed with a 1px border in `#283626`, housing deep olive text. On hover, fills smoothly with `#283626` and shifts text to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Typeset in `label-sm` with a standalone 1px hairline underline offset by 5px in antique gold (`#C5A880`). Hover expands the line opacity and smoothly transitions the text tone.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Couture Image Cards:** High-aspect ratio (3:4 or 4:5 portrait) with clean crisp perimeter cuts. Optional double-box framing inspired by the logo mark for bespoke capsule features.
- **Card Metadata:** Set cleanly beneath the imagery. Garment name rendered in `headline-sm` or `title-md`, accompanied by subtle fabric composition details in `#6C7A67` (`body-sm`) and price display in `#283626`.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist rectangular frames enclosed in a 1px border of `#D8D3C5` with a `#FAF8F5` fill. On focus, the border shifts to deep olive green (`#283626`) or antique gold (`#C5A880`) without outer halos. Field labels float above in tracked uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted 16x16px square geometries with a 1px hairline border in `#283626`. Checked status displays an inner square pip in antique gold (`#C5A880`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection Chips:** Sharp rectangular tags outlined with 1px `#D8D3C5`. Active selections transition to a soft `#EDE8DF` background with a solid `#283626` border and text.

### Bespoke Couture Modules
- **Fabric & Drape Inspection Panel:** A modular split view pairing high-resolution macro photography of linen, silk, and embroidery with artisan provenance notes and tailored sizing recommendations.
- **Monogrammed Fitting Drawer:** A right-docked drawer framed by hairline borders, providing private appointment bookings for made-to-measure consultations.
